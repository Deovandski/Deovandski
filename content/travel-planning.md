1. **When and where?** I look up the climate and the overall info about the place(s) I'm going, to decide if it's safe enough to travel, and what I need visa-wise.
2. **What are the hard commitments?** Things where I have to be at a certain place at a certain time. Those get sorted out and added to the calendar. This is also when I start digging through Tripadvisor, Viator and AI search for more hard commitments tied to group travel, events, concerts, etc.
3. **Buy the hotel and transportation.** Those are the next layer of hard commitments.
4. **Figure out how much time I have.** I split it into morning, afternoon, evening and late-night chunks, tied to where I'll be and when.
5. **Fill in the chunks.** I search for things to do around there and fill each time chunk with 1–3 things, based on proximity. If something is important or very desirable, it becomes a hard commitment. It's okay to keep a few things in the backlog, saved in case we have more time than planned. The same way, if we run out of time or energy, we can pop things out of the chunks and reorder them, or move them to the backlog for a future trip.
6. **Enjoy!** The goal is a structure that gives the trip a sense of coordination, efficient travel and relaxation, while staying flexible enough for on-the-fly adjustments.
