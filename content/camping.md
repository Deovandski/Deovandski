# Camping

Edit and push; the Camping tab rebuilds from this file.

- Each `##` heading is one campsite: write the location as the title.
- The plain text under it is the short description.
- Two photos per card: put them in `content/camping/<title>/`, using the title in
  lower case with dashes. Example: "## Example State Park, North Dakota" goes in
  `content/camping/example-state-park-north-dakota/`.
- Until a card has photos, it shows two "Photo coming soon" boxes.

Copy this block, remove the "<!--" and "-->" lines, and fill it in:

<!--
## Example State Park, North Dakota

A sentence or two about the place.
-->
