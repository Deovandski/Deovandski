- **Simon Baron-Cohen**, *Progress in Neuro-Psychopharmacology & Biological Psychiatry* (2006), "The hyper-systemizing, assortative mating theory of autism": the autistic mind is tuned to systems with firm rules (if this, then that), and struggles with things that keep changing, like the social world. [Paper](https://docs.autismresearchcentre.com/papers/2006_BC_Neuropsychophamacology.pdf)
- **Mirko Uljarević and colleagues**, *Autism* (2022): of 237 autistic kids and teens, 75% had at least one special interest, and vehicles and transportation were among the common ones. [Paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9126999/)
- **Rachel Grove, Rosa A. Hoekstra, Marlies Wierda & Sander Begeer**, *Autism Research* (2018): most autistic adults had more than one special interest, and the ones driven by them reported higher wellbeing. [Abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29427546/)
- ***State Aviation Journal***, "The Bond of Aviation and Autism": Jacob Erikson, an autistic teen whose love of plane spotting found him a welcoming community that helped his communication. [Article](https://stateaviationjournal.com/index.php/state-news/north-carolina/the-bond-of-aviation-and-autism)
