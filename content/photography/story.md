I'm no real photographer. But back in the day I got my hands on a Sony Alpha DSLR-A350, and I had a blast playing with every setting just to see what it did to the picture. Hands-on learning at its finest: fail, learn, fail some more, learn some more.

These days my camera is my Pixel 8 Pro. I still mess with the settings now and then, but I've switched to a "capture the moment" approach: most of my artistic sense comes from wanting to freeze the exact moment, from the exact angle I saw it, because it'll never look quite like that again. Where my "photographic skills" shine most is chasing depth, wide lenses, punchy colors and contrast. Also smoke and fog. I've got a thing for it. And reflections. And godrays...

There's probably more to it, so I'll let Claude weigh in below.
