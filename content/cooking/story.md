Like my mom, I don't follow recipes and I don't write stuff down. Going with the flow is very ADHD of me: [adults with ADHD tend to prefer the loose, unstructured side of creativity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7543022/). I do take pictures though so that I can try to replicate later if it turned out real good! Makes every meal special I'd say! [Churrasco](#churrasco) warrants a facet of its own! I love food of all shapes and kinds, although I do draw some lines including being unable to eat stinky tofu.

Cooking sits under Hearth because cooking at home is an essential piece that makes home, homie. Except deep fried stuff! I don't do that at home, as I have a sensitive nose. That's the AuDHD in me: [autistic adults pick up smells more easily](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4407326/), and [sensory hypersensitivity is a core part of adult ADHD too](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28371743/).

[Caleb Hammer](https://www.youtube.com/@calebhammer) would be proud that most of my meals come from my own kitchen, if we zoom into the financial aspect of it, but for me, it's about making home feel like home.
