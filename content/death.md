This is roughly how much time I have left. I'm striving to make the most of every second of it.

**Death is the greatest equalizer.** It doesn't matter how highly you think of yourself, how much you hate me, what you have that I don't, whether you envy my successes or laugh at my failures: in the end, you'll rot away exactly like I will.

## Take care of your mind

Since we're on the subject: take care of your mental health. And if you've ever thought about ending it all... don't. I nearly did, back in 2016, during a period that far surpassed what my mind could take at the time. I called a crisis hotline. To the older lady who picked up the phone that night: I will forever be grateful. Like many people who walk away from that temptation, I'm so glad I didn't go through with it. I used it to build stronger immunity against depression, and much later I discovered that what I'd been fighting included the negative sides of AuDHD. **Now nothing short of death itself will stop me.**

<div class="help">

If you're struggling right now, you don't have to carry it alone. I didn't. In the US, call or text **988**, or chat at [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org/). Anywhere else, [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com/) lists free, confidential helplines in your country.

</div>

## Death as my compass

Resilience is built, not born, and even the worst moments can be fuel for growth. I've faced death a few times already, and I'm no longer afraid of it. Instead, I use that undeniable fact as a compass: it steers me to do the right thing, always, and to chase what makes me fulfilled and happy. **Nobody gets to dictate what my life should be.** I'll meet in the middle and compromise, but I won't sacrifice my life. I know what it's worth.

## Your clock is ticking too

You're human, like me. We have different capabilities, yes, but nothing stops you from picturing your own death clock and starting to work on your life today. **You can't change the past, but you can do something right now to build a better future.**
