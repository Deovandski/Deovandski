## Take care of your mind

Since we're on the subject: take care of your mental health. And if you've ever thought about ending it all... don't. I nearly did, back in 2016, during a period that far surpassed what my mind could take at the time. I called a crisis hotline. To the older lady who picked up the phone that night: I will forever be grateful. The "Harry" you see in Vol. 1 is an alternate version of myself: the one who plunged that knife into his chest that night.

Why share something so dark and personal? <mark>Simple: I hope this finds the right person at the right moment.</mark> I was privileged to have a great, supportive family, but not everyone has that, so this is my way of putting positive energy into this weird world. Take care of yourself. If you suspect you're on the spectrum and want someone who understands, or want to hear it from someone like me, feel free to [reach out](contact/). But if you're going through an active crisis, don't be afraid to reach out for professional help.

## Death as my compass

Like many people who walk away from that temptation, I'm so glad I didn't go through with it. I used it to build stronger immunity against depression and my internal cycles, and in 2026 I discovered that much of what I'd been fighting was the negative side of AuDHD: the masking itself was corroding my soul and draining its energy every single day. **Now nothing short of a bullet to my head, or death itself coming to my door and whisking me away to whatever comes next, will stop me from pursuing a fulfilling life.**

Resilience is built, not born, and even the worst moments can be fuel for growth. I've faced death a few times already, and I'm no longer afraid of it. Instead, I use that undeniable fact as a compass: it steers me to do the right thing, always, and to chase what makes me fulfilled and happy. **Nobody gets to dictate what my life should be.** I'll meet in the middle and compromise, but I won't sacrifice my life. I know what it's worth.

## Your clock is ticking too

You're human, like me. We have different capabilities, yes, but nothing stops you from picturing your own death clock and starting to work on your life today. **You can't change the past, but you can do something right now to build a better future.**

I'm not a nihilist, and I don't indulge in the self-masturbatory idea that nothing matters. I reverse-engineered my way of life on the **[🔥 Mentor tab](#mentor)** from one core fact, that I will perish, to build a foundation that brings me the most fulfillment, so that in the end there are no regrets.
