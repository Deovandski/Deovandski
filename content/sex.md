As a citizen of the world, I've experienced many cultures, and I developed my own moral compass from two facts: I'm mortal, and I'm curious. 

I've had the pleasure of sharing intimacy with quite a few women, from pillow princesses where I had to do everything, to dominant girls who went on to do as they pleased. Regardless, I learned a lot about what brings me joy, and how to help bring them joy too. Due to being AuDHD and becoming shameless over my human carnal desires, I literally showed an interested girl a checklist of things I wanted to try, complete with reference docs. She said she could teach me even more, so we spent three hours going through every item and ranking it in a very scientific way to help me write Vols 2 and 3 of my series and improve my knowledge of the subject. Most of those were one-night stands; a few extended to multiple nights, as I enjoyed cuddling, but none became romantically entangled. 

Now that the novelty has worn off, I truly see sex as one of the pillars of a healthy relationship, alongside communication, trust, and respect. I have yet to find a woman I'd trust with my life and my children, someone ready to commit for life. Until that day comes, and we commit to each other on a soul level, and strive to meet in the middle and choose each other every day, I'll keep pursuing my fantasies and my curiosity.

My advice to anyone who stumbles onto this: **don't use sex as a carrot on a stick.** Turning it into a reward to keep a partner in line is disgusting. Not being in the mood, or just asking to cuddle, is one thing; I myself told a girl the morning after that I just wanted to have breakfast and cuddle, and I've been told that too... But withholding intimacy, physical or emotional, to reward or punish is fucking disgusting. If that makes you defensive, look inward at the type of partner you attract and how you behave. You harvest what you sow.

Anyways, if you came here expecting graphic details because I'm AuDHD and very transparent... no. I have boundaries. I'm transparent about myself, but I respect others' privacy.
