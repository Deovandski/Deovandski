1. Sword Art Online
Very controversial, yes. But I have to give it #1 because of what it meant to me at a specific point in my life. In March 2019 I was stagnating, going through the motions: depressed, heavily masked, and unable to break the loop. I wasn't even traveling. Just work, eat, shit, sleep. Gaming and fishing were nice, but they only scratched the surface; something deeper was missing. I hadn't watched many anime before this (Bleach, AOT, Yosuga no Sora, To LOVE-Ru), so this one hit me like a freight train and triggered hyperfocus. It even breathed life back into me to do better at the gym (back then I used the gym to de-stress rather than truly enjoying being there). This anime was one of the cornerstones, alongside the Vraki writing I had done before, of what happened in October 2019: the #HusbandOutWorld project.

2. Bleach
Back at Moderno High School in Macapá, someone handed me a DVD with "Bleach S1 pt-br sub" written on it in Sharpie. I was so curious I nearly ran from school to my mother's store, did what I had to do on the server and in the store, then sat down in the back office, dropped the DVD into the tray and hit play. The first line of the opening song, Orange Range's "Asterisk", got burned into my brain as a core memory. It's 2026, and I still remember that moment extremely vividly.

3. Yosuga no Sora (In Solitude, Where We Are Least Alone)
Yep. That anime tickled something deep and turbulent that I didn't understand back then: my ecchi/hentai energy. I truly understand it now, and I have no shame about it, since it's something I set out to explore to feel more fulfilled.

4. Shingeki no Kyojin (Attack on Titan)
I guess the real #1. The ending is complicated, but more power to the author for sticking to what he wanted to do. When I first watched Season 1 back in 2014, I literally had to sleep with the lights on because it haunted my imagination. I also love how rich the world building is. It reminds me of Age of Empires II, where I'd build a custom map with my city fortified with Imperial Age walls, focus on my own thing on my own time, and then, when I was ready, open the gates and slaughter the other empires that had been bickering with each other the whole time. Not exactly the same, but that unawareness of the wider world is an interesting phenomenon. Amazing OPs, too!

5. Mushoku Tensei: Isekai Ittara Honki Dasu (Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation)
Love it. Even the ecchi. Everything has a purpose, and I have nothing to complain about. If I had watched Mushoku Tensei before writing #HusbandOutWorld, I might not have written my story at all, because it's good enough to quench a lot of the thirst I had while consuming one isekai after another.

6. Made in Abyss
The juxtaposition between what you think it's about and what actually happens reminds me of Happy Tree Friends. The world lore and its rules are fascinating!

7. Steins;Gate
I'm usually not a fan of multiple timelines and time machines, but this is clearly an exception. The whole thing is crafted like a finely tuned orchestra, and the pieces fall into place perfectly.

8. Re:Zero kara Hajimeru Isekai Seikatsu (Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World)
So intense, so powerful. Loved it! The later seasons try to update the formula, but I think it got too complex for its own good, with too many people to track and a bit of a villain-of-the-week problem. Regardless: Rem, best girl!

9. Kono Subarashii Sekai ni Shukufuku wo! (KonoSuba: God's Blessing on This Wonderful World!)
If you read my Vol 2 afterword, you'll understand why this one is so meaningful to me. Besides loving Megumin and the rest of the cast, I love how it takes potshots at the entire isekai genre, and the laissez-faire, shitposting attitude it brings.

10. To LOVE-Ru
Ecchi in the shape of an anime. I absolutely loved the creativity in pushing the envelope as far as possible! Yami is my favorite girl, even though IRL that type of girl tends to be toxic as fuck. Avoidant girls, especially fearful avoidants, are complete energy vampires.

11. Youjo Senki (Saga of Tanya the Evil)
I love how rich the world lore is, and also how deep it goes into the mechanics of war. Great soundtrack, too!

12. Ishuzoku Reviewers (Interspecies Reviewers)
I was laughing out loud at how committed and no-shits-given they were to the bit. That's true artistic integrity.

13. Cyberpunk: Edgerunners
That one really got me in the feels. It made me feel more in one sitting than I felt while dating some girls. It's hard for me to miss someone once I say goodbye, but this anime made me sit with that feeling for a good while after watching it.

14. Kaguya-sama wa Kokurasetai (Kaguya-sama: Love Is War)
I love how alive the characters are and how creative the shenanigans get, even though I absolutely hate people who "play hard to get". As we say in Portuguese: "Quem faz cu doce só atrai formiga e vagabundo" (whoever plays hard to get only attracts ants and bums).

15. Spy x Family
Based on what you've seen so far, you might expect me not to watch this, but nope. I love how grounded it feels, and the plot does a really good job of using its characters. Cute jokes, too. I'll be showing this to my future kids for sure.

16. Kimetsu no Yaiba (Demon Slayer)
Part of me wondered about ranking it higher, but it's tough, since a lot of anime have a closer, more special meaning to me. The animation gets a standing ovation, and so do the story and plot development!

17. Monogatari
This one is for seasoned veterans of the genre, or someone deep into Japanese culture. The unique way the story is told is still vivid in my brain, despite all the anime I've absorbed since.

18. Death Note
I've learned a lot more about narcissism recently, and boy, Light Yagami was really well written, alongside the other characters.

19. Mob Psycho 100
The story development, the world lore and the characters themselves are really well done! That ending was unique; I still remember pieces of it.

20. Sono Bisque Doll wa Koi wo Suru (My Dress-Up Darling)
So good it brought back memories of how the initial spark went in my relationships, except this anime takes a better path, while I kept getting attracted to mysterious girls (à la Darkness from To LOVE-Ru) and ending up with a broken heart every time. At least I've learned my lesson now.

21. Toaru Kagaku no Railgun (A Certain Scientific Railgun)
Misaki is hands down my favorite character in the entire series! And this is my favorite series in the "A Certain..." world.

22. Nanatsu no Taizai (The Seven Deadly Sins)
Memorable characters, a story that moves, people who grow, a plot that thickens, and interesting world building. For how long it is, it's mostly sensible.

23. Toradora!
I mean, it's Taiga! But for real, the chemistry between the two main characters is well developed, which is why so many people still remember it.

24. Akira
A true classic. It's been years since I watched it, but I can close my eyes and still see flashbacks of how detailed it was.

25. Hagane no Renkinjutsushi: Fullmetal Alchemist (Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood)
I remember binge-watching the whole thing in a few days. Solid story, characters and world lore.

26. Goblin Slayer
They really dove deep into D&D with a unique take on the world lore, and you know what? Season 1 was truly memorable.

27. Ookami to Koushinryou (Spice and Wolf)
Besides the relationship and the bonding, the merchant life and bartering are really well developed! I took inspiration from it while working on the world lore for #HusbandOutWorld.

28. Another
My entry point to the more morbid and twisted stuff like When They Cry, so it has a special place in my mind.

29. Higurashi no Naku Koro ni (When They Cry)
I watched the remake. Spoiler warning, but goddamn, can these two just fucking rub clits and get over this shit? ...huehuehuehue. Okay, shitposting aside, the scene with the rake is so fucking insane it still haunts me years later. I slept fine, but it was an assault on the senses... Well done.

30. Chuunibyou demo Koi ga Shitai! (Love, Chunibyo & Other Delusions!)
So damn cute! It reminded me of going through adolescence, which means it does a really good job of capturing how weird and fascinating it is to grow into early adulthood. Also, I absolutely love Satone, because I love clingy girls!

31. Log Horizon
Not many people have heard of this isekai, but honestly, it's a top dog in the genre for really getting into the weeds of its world building and trying to figure out how everything works.

32. Sakurasou no Pet na Kanojo (The Pet Girl of Sakurasou)
This anime reminded me of a girl I got close to once. I was undiagnosed autistic, and she was even more autistic than me. We couldn't figure it out, she had tons of baggage, and you can't fix someone else's shit.

33. Mahou Shoujo Madoka Magica (Puella Magi Madoka Magica)
The kind of anime that makes you go "Oh! Didn't expect that!", with a unique creative vision for portraying the struggles and feelings of adolescence. Unique!

34. Tokyo Ghoul
The later seasons kind of derailed it, but that first season got me hooked, and I binge-watched it in one sitting. The MC's progression and changing persona is something I took notes on, and you can see it in Harry and other characters.

35. Fate/stay night & Fate/Zero
Shoutout to Unlimited Blade Works, and especially Heaven's Feel. Grounded world rules, and each branch of the story is truly unique.

36. Seikon no Qwaser (The Qwaser of Stigmata)
I watched this in the same time frame as Yosuga no Sora. Interesting world rules, and definitely a fetish for sucking tits. It made me curious about being physically intimate with a commanding, forward girl... Results are in! It's fun, given I'm usually the more dominant type.

37. Nichijou (My Ordinary Life) & Non Non Biyori
The complete opposite of some of the other stuff I watched, but I have fond memories of them. So cute and chill!

38. Overlord
One of my favorite isekai, and one of the OGs from before the torrent of copycats trying to milk the market dry.

39. Isekai Shokudou (Restaurant to Another World)
It's rare for me to watch something, get hungry, go find the ingredients and immediately try to recreate a dish from the show. That beef stew was divine!
