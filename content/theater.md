# Theater & Opera Facet

Edit and push; the Theater & Opera tab rebuilds from this file. It works like
the Concert tab (content/concerts.md).

- Each `##` heading is a kind of show. Each bullet is one show; add the
  company or venue after a colon, e.g. `- Hamilton: CIBC Theatre, Chicago`.
- Kinds and shows are sorted alphabetically on the site automatically, so
  the order here doesn't matter.
- Tags go in braces after the name: `- Hamilton {Favorite}`.
- Show images: drop a square-ish photo in `content/theater/shows/`, named
  after the show in lower case with dashes ("&" becomes "and"), e.g.
  `hamilton.jpg`, `madama-butterfly.jpg`. It shows as a circle left of
  the name; until then the circle shows initials.
- Plain text under a heading is shown as that kind's description.
