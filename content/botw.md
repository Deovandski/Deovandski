# Best of the Worst

Edit and push; the BOTW tab rebuilds from this file.

- `>` lines are the text above the list.
- Number each pick and give it as a Markdown link to the YouTube video:
  `1. [Best of the Worst: Example Episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...)`.
  A bare link (`1. https://youtu.be/...`) works too; it's then titled "Best of the Worst #1".
- Optional: lines under a pick (until the next number) are a short note about it.
- Up to 39 picks; until there are any, the tab says they're on their way.
