# Approximate dates

For pictures with no date of their own (no photo metadata, no date in the filename).
Each line is `- <filename>: YYYY-MM-DD`. The picture is shown as "Circa Q<quarter> <year>"
and sorted by that day. A year alone (`- <filename>: 2013`) shows as "Circa 2013". A real date (metadata or filename) always wins over this list.
