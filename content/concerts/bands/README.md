# Band images

One image per band, named after the band as it appears in `content/concerts.md`,
in lower case with dashes ("&" becomes "and"):

    ghost.jpg
    evanescence.png
    munly-and-the-lupercalians.jpg
    black-jacket-symphony.webp
    the-witcher-in-concert.jpg

Square images look best (they're cropped to a circle). Formats: .jpg, .jpeg,
.png, .webp, .avif. Until a band has an image, its circle shows its initials.
