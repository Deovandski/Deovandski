## The mantras

<div class="mantras">

- Whatever you do, or don't, is a seed you will harvest. Mistakes and knowledge are the fertilizer for a stronger one.
- Either full commit or eat shit.
- I don't let it happen. I make it happen.
- I'm growing, and I will keep growing until the day I die.
- Silence is the poison of the soul instead of communication, assuming you still have faith in the other person.
- Never wrestle with pigs. You both get dirty, and the pig likes it. <span class="newest">New</span>

## Hard truths

- **Movement creates evidence.** Evidence updates the model; the mission continues, but the model stays editable. Refuse limbo, and prefer an honest no to a manipulative maybe.
- Learn hard things. Enter rooms bigger than your competence. Never confuse experienced with finished. Repetition makes habits; deliberate correction makes skill. **Comfort may visit me. It will never become my god.**
- Pain doesn't make you wiser, and it isn't proof of growth; changed behavior is. Time doesn't heal you. Information doesn't become behavior. **A lesson that never changes a decision was entertainment.**
- Nobody can commit for you. People can open doors. **Nobody walks through them using your legs.**
- Potential gets encouragement and wisdom from me, never applause. **Applause is for the first real step from potential to reality.** A dream earns dignity when your behavior starts serving it.
- Dream violently. Plan soberly. Execute relentlessly. Update honestly. **If the evidence stabilizes and resistance drops, execute.** Intention without implementation is self-entertainment.
- **A signal is not a steering wheel.** Feelings and hunches are telemetry: observe, classify, keep moving. When action becomes obvious and resistance drops to zero, check it against your values, then move.
- Confidence is not noise. **It's preparation remembering what it has survived.** Past wins prove capability, not entitlement. Build boldly, measure honestly, correct without ego.
- **Say what you mean. Do what you say. If your direction changes, own it.** I can work with truth; I won't build on fog. Don't control me, challenge me. Don't flatter me, engage me. Don't promise me, show me.
- **Peace is not passivity.** Peace is maintained: boundaries, confrontation, recovery, honest decisions. A boundary without enforcement is just a request.
- Spiritual bypassing is still bypassing. Hiding behind Buddhist quotes, "just let it go," or standing by idle while someone chooses self-destruction isn't peace; it's what Chögyam Trungpa called idiot compassion. **Say your piece, then walk away.**
- Love is not an exemption from standards. **Compassion can explain behavior without volunteering to absorb it.**
- Access is earned and maintained through behavior. Curiosity opens the door; consistency earns access. Nobody gets root access because they asked. **I will meet you in the middle. I will not live there alone.**
- Time, energy, attention and trust are finite capital. Stop subsidizing what keeps damaging you. **My attention is not public property.**
- Not every battle deserves you. Sometimes "persistence" is just fear of admitting the system is dead. **Fight in faith for a better future. When people prove unworthy of your love, losing that faith isn't failure; it's clarity.**
- **Full commitment is not eternal commitment.** If there's movement, I can work with imperfection. If the system only circles, I stop feeding it. I fight for living systems. I bury dead ones.
- Build things worth maintaining. Leave systems healthier than you found them. **Achievement asks what I can win. Stewardship asks what I can keep alive.**
- Engineer and artist. Founder and teammate. Traveler and home-builder. Intense builder and quiet cat dad. **I refuse to amputate parts of myself to become easier to classify.**

<details class="sources">
<summary>Shoulders I stand on</summary>

- **Aristotle**, *Nicomachean Ethics*, Book II: we become just by doing just acts. Character is built by repeated action. [Text](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.2.ii.html)
- **Theodore Roosevelt**, "The Strenuous Life," Hamilton Club, Chicago, April 10, 1899: "not the doctrine of ignoble ease, but the doctrine of the strenuous life." The speech was also an argument for American empire; what I take from it is the personal part: choose worthy difficulty. [Text](https://voicesofdemocracy.umd.edu/?p=927)
- **Edward Deci & Richard Ryan**, Self-Determination Theory: autonomy, competence and relatedness as basic psychological needs. [Overview](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/theory/)
- **Steven C. Hayes**, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy's psychological flexibility: stay present with what you feel, and persist or change in service of chosen values. [ACBS](https://contextualscience.org/the_six_core_processes_of_act)
- **Peter Gollwitzer**, implementation intentions ("if X happens, then I will do Y"). His 2006 meta-analysis with Paschal Sheeran, across 94 tests, found a medium-to-large effect on reaching goals. [Paper](https://www.decisionskills.com/uploads/5/1/6/0/5160560/2006_gollwitzersheeran_implementation_intentions.pdf)
- **Gabriele Oettingen**, mental contrasting and WOOP: wish, outcome, obstacle, plan. [WOOP](https://woopmylife.org/)
- **K. Anders Ericsson, Ralf Krampe & Clemens Tesch-Römer** (1993), deliberate practice. Honest update: Brooke Macnamara and Megha Maitra's 2019 replication found practice matters, but much less than the original study claimed. [Replication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6731745/)
- **Carsten Wrosch and colleagues** (2003): letting go of unattainable goals and reengaging with new ones is linked to higher well-being. [Paper](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/psychology/pdf/scales/GAS_article.pdf)
- **John Welwood**, Buddhist teacher and psychotherapist, coined "spiritual bypassing" in 1984: using spiritual ideas and practices to sidestep unresolved emotional issues and unfinished work. [Interview](http://www.johnwelwood.com/articles/TRIC_interview_uncut.pdf)
- **Chögyam Trungpa**, Tibetan Buddhist teacher, coined "idiot compassion": kindness that avoids conflict. His student Pema Chödrön describes it as enabling. [Pema Chödrön](https://tricycle.org/?p=1701)
- **Erik Erikson**, generativity: caring for what will outlive you. Its virtue is care. [Reference](https://link.springer.com/referenceworkentry/10.1007/978-0-387-79061-9_1218)
- **John Wooden**, Pyramid of Success: poise and confidence sit near the top, built on every block beneath; competitive greatness is the peak. [Pyramid](https://coachwooden.com/pyramid-of-success)

</details>
