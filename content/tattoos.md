# Tattoo Facet

Edit and push; the Tattoo Facet tab rebuilds from this file.

- Lines starting with `>` before the first area are the intro at the top of the tab.
- Each `##` heading is a body area. Its photos go in `content/tattoos/<area>/`,
  with the area in lower case and dashes, e.g. `content/tattoos/left-forearm/`.
- Plain text under a heading (not a bullet) is shown as that area's intro.
- Each bullet is one tattoo: an optional symbol, the name, a colon, the meaning.
  Start a bullet with `[vraki]` to also show its name in the Vraki script.
- Indented lines under a bullet become extra paragraphs for that tattoo.

> None of my tattoos are perfect, and that's part of what makes them special. The imperfections are what make them human.

## Left forearm

- ᚹ Wunjo: Nordic rune commonly associated with joy, harmony, fulfillment, and well-being.
- ᛁ Isa: Nordic rune associated with ice, stillness, focus, containment, and preservation.
- ᚷ Gebo: Nordic rune representing gift, exchange, reciprocity, partnership, and mutual obligations.
- ᛟ Othala: Nordic rune associated with heritage, ancestry, inheritance, homeland, and legacy.
  Mine isn't fully aligned with the other three, and I gave that a meaning too: some of my memories are misaligned. This weird life of mine, like everyone else's, is about figuring out who I am, why I'm alive, and what I want from it before I perish.

## Right forearm

- 初音ミク Hatsune Miku: Miku's name in Japanese. Miku-chan (ミクちゃん) is a cornerstone of my life, tied to the things that drive me: ambition, technology, collaboration and community. She also stands for my passion, my drive, and the anxiety side of my personality.
  The tattoo is mirrored. That used to bring me shame, until I realized it's a matter of perspective: under some conditions it looks perfect, and under others it doesn't. That gap became a lesson in mindset and in how you choose to see things.

## Wrists

Both wrists are written in Vraki. I always dreamed of coming up with a language: as a kid I doodled the alphabets of alien civilizations, and the more I traveled and soaked up the world's cultures, the closer those doodles drifted to Vraki. It went digital as a little Windows app in 2017, grew into the writing of the whole #HusbandOutWorld world, and now it's part of my skin. Vraki is written 1:1 with English, so anyone who learns it can read my wrists.

- [vraki] Be Awesome!: Left wrist.
- [vraki] Do Better!: Right wrist.

## Chest

- Vegvísir: The Icelandic compass symbol, associated with guidance and finding one's way. I intentionally placed its center point over a single long white hair that I consider mystical.
