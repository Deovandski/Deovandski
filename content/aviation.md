With my autistic brain, it's no surprise I love aviation. I always loved planes and was going to be a pilot. But my parents worked in the industry and worried they were the ones steering me there. I grew up around it, even helping my mom on the SABRE system so we could go home sooner. They did the right thing by pushing me to make my own decision… and I ended up in software development.

I still love aviation, though. The complexity of a cockpit and the endless systems to hyperfocus on keep pulling me back. That's why I love Mentour Pilot's videos: he gets deep into the weeds of airplane components while breaking down accident investigations.

A private pilot license is still in my queue! In the meantime, I want to build a home cockpit simulator once I buy a house. In Singapore, I got to fly a full-scale replica of a Boeing 737-800NG cockpit, and it became a core memory. It took me back to my early teens, when I rode in the cockpit jump seat of an Airbus A320-233 taking off from Santos Dumont Airport (SDU) in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
