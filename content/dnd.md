# D&D

Edit and push; the D&D tab rebuilds from this file.

- Each `##` heading is one card (a campaign, a character, a table...).
- The plain text under it is the short description.
- Two photos per card: put them in `content/dnd/<title>/`, using the title in
  lower case with dashes ("&" becomes "and"). Example: "## Vraki West March"
  goes in `content/dnd/vraki-west-march/`.
- Until a card has photos, it shows two "Photo coming soon" boxes.

Copy this block for each card, remove the "<!--" and "-->" lines, and fill it in:

<!--
## Card title

A sentence or two about it.
-->
