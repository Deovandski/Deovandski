# Fishing

Edit and push; the Fishing tab rebuilds from this file.

- Each `##` heading is one spot: write the location as the title.
- The plain text under it is the short description.
- Two photos per card: put them in `content/fishing/<title>/`, using the title in
  lower case with dashes. Example: "## Lake Example, Minnesota" goes in
  `content/fishing/lake-example-minnesota/`.
- Until a card has photos, it shows two "Photo coming soon" boxes.

Copy this block, remove the "<!--" and "-->" lines, and fill it in:

## Lake Example, Minnesota

A sentence or two about the place.
-->
