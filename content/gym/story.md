For years, the gym was a pressure valve. I went for stress relief, so it never gave me satisfaction: it was a tool for relief, not something I enjoyed. Part of that was my undiagnosed AuDHD, and not realizing how much masking I was doing every single day.

In 2025, a girl became the catalyst. I took the hypervigilance, mental abuse and misery I was put through and turned it into massive growth and knowledge. For the first time in my life, going to the gym is as natural as brushing my teeth. I don't go to relieve stress anymore; I go because I actually enjoy it, listening to music on the elliptical or while lifting.

Wake up at 6am, drink coffee, poop, bathe, and hit the gym before work. It's a practical, frictionless part of my routine now, not something I have to force myself to do.

## Steps I've taken

- Increased my protein intake.
- Fixed my fatty liver.
- Made the gym a place of zen for the body.
- Started going in the mornings, at 6:30am.

## Now

Scaling up weight training and chasing 200 lb. Sometimes the weight swings, and that's part of the journey.

**Onward to 200 lb!**
