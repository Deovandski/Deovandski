For years, the gym was a pressure valve. I went for stress relief, so it never gave me satisfaction: it was a tool for relief, not something I enjoyed. Part of that was my undiagnosed AuDHD, and not realizing how much masking I was doing every single day.

In 2025, a "special girl 💜", as I called her in my [Vol. 3 afterword](https://deovandski.github.io/HusbandOutWorld/about/afterwords/#vol-3-afterword-2026) at the time, became the catalyst for a quantum leap. Back then, I thanked her for being a catalyst for change. Looking back, the change came from what I did with everything that followed: I took the hypervigilance, mental abuse and misery I was put through and turned it into massive growth and knowledge. For the first time in my life, going to the gym is as natural as brushing my teeth. I don't go to relieve stress anymore; I go because I actually enjoy it, listening to music on the elliptical or while lifting.

Wake up at 6am, drink coffee, poop, bathe, hit the gym, then head home to let Peeta run in the backyard while I shove food in my piehole before work. It's a practical, frictionless part of my routine now, not something I have to force myself to do.

## Steps I've taken

- Increased my protein intake.
- Fixed my fatty liver.
- Made the gym a place of zen for the body.
- Started going in the mornings, at 6:30am.

## Now

Scaling up weight training and chasing 200 lb. Sometimes the weight swings, and that's part of the journey.

**Onward to 200 lb!**
