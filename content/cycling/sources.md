- **The Lancet Child & Adolescent Health** (2025), "The sleep–circadian connection: pathways to understanding and supporting autism and attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder": insomnia, a delayed sleep–wake phase and restless legs are far more common in autistic people and people with ADHD than in neurotypical peers. [Review](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/507892/)
- **Theodore Petti, Mayank Gupta, Yuli Fradkin & Nihit Gupta**, *BJPsych Open* (2023), sleep disorders in autism with co-occurring ADHD: sleep disruption shows up mostly as insomnia and a body clock out of step with the day, and having both brings more disruption than either one alone. [Paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10755553/)
- ***BMC Public Health*** (2025), "The role of sunlight in sleep regulation": the midpoint of sleep moved most with sunlight, above all with morning sun. [Abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41053799/)
- **M. Alexandra Kredlow and colleagues**, *Journal of Behavioral Medicine* (2015), a meta-analysis of physical activity and sleep: regular exercise has small benefits for total sleep time, small-to-medium ones for falling asleep, and moderate ones for sleep quality. [Abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25596964/)
- **Yi Xie and colleagues**, *Frontiers in Psychiatry* (2021), a meta-analysis of 22 randomized trials (cycling among the exercises): regular exercise mainly improves how well people say they sleep. [Paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8215288/)
- **Rebecca Hymas, Johanna C. Badcock & Elizabeth Milne**, *Review Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders* (2024), "Loneliness in Autism and Its Association with Anxiety and Depression": across 23 studies, autistic people reported much more loneliness than neurotypical people, and loneliness went with anxiety and, more strongly, depression. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40489-022-00330-w)
- **Jack Lear, Diana Dorstyn, Melissa Opozda & Ian Zajac**, *Review Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders* (2026), "Loneliness and Social Connection in Autistic and/or ADHD Males": across 82 studies, autistic and ADHD males were as socially connected as neurotypical males or less, never more, and less connection went with worse mental health. [Review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40489-026-00542-4)
- **Yi Yang and colleagues**, *Journal of Global Health* (2025), a meta-analysis of physical activity and inhibitory control in adults with ADHD: both single sessions and regular exercise improved it, regular exercise the most. [Paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11907377/)
- **Shuqi Jia and colleagues**, *Frontiers in Pediatrics* (2023), a meta-analysis of 14 randomized trials: exercise improved social communication in autistic people, most of all in programs with several participants that ran longer. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3389/fped.2023.1193648)
