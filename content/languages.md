# Multilingual Life

Edit the table and push; the equalizer in the Multilingual Life tab rebuilds from it.

- Level: 1 to 10, how many bars light up.
- Color: green, yellow or red.
- Note: a short line shown under the bar.

| Language | Native | Level | Color | Note |
|----------|--------|-------|-------|------|
| English | English | 10 | green | Fully bilingual |
| Portuguese | Português | 10 | green | Fully bilingual |
| Spanish | Español | 6 | yellow | Getting there |
| Japanese | 日本語 | 3 | red | Learning |
