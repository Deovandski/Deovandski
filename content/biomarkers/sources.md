- **Ultrahuman**, "HRV by age and gender": men's median overnight HRV (RMSSD) by decade, 52 ms in their 20s, 45 in their 30s, 40 in their 40s, from over 510,000 ring users. [Article](https://www.ultrahuman.com/blog/hrv-by-age-and-gender-whats-normal-for-men-vs-women/)
